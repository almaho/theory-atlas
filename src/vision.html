OUR VISION

Think beyond
one problem.

As AI advances, theoretical computer science needs a broader view of its ideas—and clearer ways for people to learn new results.

We want to make it easier to move between problems, models, and techniques, and to recognize the structure they share. Looking at problems more abstractly can reveal connections that are difficult to see when each result is studied in isolation.

Theory Atlas is an attempt toward that way of thinking: a visual place to understand what is known, compare the assumptions behind results, and ask what the broader picture suggests.

01

Broaden the view

See a result in the context of other problems and computational models. Put algorithms, lower bounds, and open questions close enough to compare.

02

Make ideas easier to learn

Give newcomers a way into unfamiliar results through clear figures, visible assumptions, and direct paths to the original papers.

03

Think in abstractions

Look for shared structures and reusable ideas. Ask which features of a problem matter, and which insights might carry to another setting.

A SHARED PROCESS

This should be built
by our community.

The abstractions we use—and the way we explain them—should grow through discussion across the TCS community. Researchers and students can help choose useful comparisons, check results, improve figures, and connect ideas across areas.

AI can support the work of finding and organizing knowledge. Community participation gives this effort its direction, judgment, and shared understanding.
